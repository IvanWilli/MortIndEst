# Package index

## All functions

- [`Orphanhood_DB`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/Orphanhood_DB.md)
  : Orphanhood database

- [`all_censuses`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/all_censuses.md)
  : Census population database

- [`census_method()`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/census_method.md)
  : Intercensal survival estimates of 15q60 based on Li-Gerland (2012).

- [`coherent_mx()`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/coherent_mx.md)
  : Extrapolate mortality rates coherently at older ages

- [`egm()`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/egm.md) :
  Extinct Generation Method (EGM) from Lexis-square deaths

- [`im_brass()`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/im_brass.md)
  : Estimate child mortality with the Brass indirect method

- [`intercensal_surv_Preston_logit_var_r()`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/intercensal_surv_Preston_logit_var_r.md)
  :

  Estimate life expectancy at age 5 (`e(5)`) using cohort relationship
  between two censuses, based on r-variable methods.

- [`intercensal_survival()`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/intercensal_survival.md)
  : Estimate adult mortality using cohort relationship between two
  censuses.

- [`lt5_wpp24_brazil`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/lt5_wpp24_brazil.md)
  : WPP 2024 five-year life tables for Brazil

- [`lt_extrap_constrained()`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/lt_extrap_constrained.md)
  : Extrapolate mortality by age, constrained to a life expectancy
  value.

- [`mac_wpp22`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/mac_wpp22.md)
  : WPP 2022 mean age of childbearing

- [`mods.r`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/mods.r.md)
  : Spectrum mortality models

- [`orphanhood_one()`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/orphanhood_one.md)
  : Estimate adult mortality with one survey/census from orphanhood
  data.

- [`orphanhood_two()`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/orphanhood_two.md)
  : Estimate adult mortality with two survey/census observations from
  orphanhood data.

- [`siblings_one()`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/siblings_one.md)
  : Estimate adult mortality with one survey/census from sibling
  survival data.

- [`widowhood_one()`](https://ivanwilli.github.io/MortIndEst/reference/widowhood_one.md)
  : Estimate adult mortality with one survey/census from widowhood data.
